## 🌍 What Is DEEPEX?

**DEEPEX is a student-focused learning platform designed to bring technical learning and professional development together.**

Learning is not just about understanding concepts or memorizing information. It is also about applying knowledge, communicating ideas, solving problems, presenting yourself confidently, and preparing for real-world opportunities.

DEEPEX brings these learning goals together through two complementary components:

- 💻 **DEEP CODE** — Technical and programming skills.
- 🗣️ **DEEP SPEAK** — Communication, interview, workplace, professional, and personal skills.

The platform aims to make learning accessible, practical, and understandable, particularly for students who benefit from learning in both Tamil and English.

**Our vision is simple: help students learn what they need, understand why it matters, and develop the confidence to apply it.**

🌐 **Explore DEEPEX:** https://deepex.vercel.app/

---

## 💡 The Idea Behind DEEPEX

Every student deserves access to learning resources that are easy to understand and useful beyond the classroom.

However, students may face different challenges:

- Understanding technical concepts for the first time.
- Learning programming without getting overwhelmed by syntax.
- Explaining projects, ideas, and knowledge clearly.
- Communicating confidently in professional situations.
- Preparing for interviews and entering the workplace.
- Learning professional expectations without prior exposure to corporate environments.

DEEPEX is being developed to address these challenges through a practical, student-friendly learning experience.

Instead of treating technical knowledge and professional skills as unrelated subjects, DEEPEX brings them together under one platform.

> **Learn technical skills. Develop communication skills. Build the confidence to grow.**

## ✨ Key Features

### 💻 Technical Learning — DEEP CODE

- **Bilingual learning:** Learn programming concepts through English and Tamil explanations.
- **Concept-based learning:** Understand definitions, syntax, examples, and program outputs.
- **Real-world applications:** Explore how programming concepts are used in practical situations.
- **Coding practice:** Work through structured programming questions, hints, expected outputs, and solutions where provided.
- **Topic search:** Find learning topics quickly.
- **Multiple programming languages:** Learn Java, Python, and C++.
- **Language switching:** Switch between programming languages instead of being permanently restricted to one.
- **Independent language progress:** Track completed topics separately for Java, Python, and C++.
- **Learning support:** Access mentor support, setup guidance, and a Things to Remember section.
- **Student-oriented navigation:** Move between the Learning Hub, topics, and coding questions.

### 🗣️ Professional Development — DEEP SPEAK

DEEP SPEAK is centred on five learning areas:

| Area | Learning focus |
|---|---|
| 🗣️ Communication Skills | Expressing ideas clearly, listening, asking questions, and communicating effectively |
| 🎤 Interview Skills | Self-introductions, answering interview questions, and explaining projects or experience |
| 💼 Workplace Skills | Teamwork, professional conversations, asking for help, and handling feedback |
| 💻 Professional Skills | Professional email, resumes, portfolios, and presenting yourself professionally |
| 🧠 Personal Skills | Time management, problem-solving, consistency, decision-making, and handling challenges |

The intended learning approach is practical and beginner-friendly: understand a situation, learn the appropriate response, see useful examples, and practise applying the skill.

The initial rollout is focused on department students. The curriculum and features will continue to evolve based on student needs and feedback.

## 🇮🇳 Designed With Bilingual Learning in Mind

For many students, learning a new skill becomes easier when the explanation is available in a language they understand well.

DEEPEX embraces a Tamil-English learning approach to help students understand concepts and gradually become more comfortable using English in academic and professional situations.

The goal is not simply to translate content. It is to make learning understandable and useful.

This approach is especially relevant to:

- Tamil-medium students.
- Beginners learning programming.
- Students building confidence in professional English.
- First-generation graduates navigating unfamiliar professional expectations.
- Students looking for practical, accessible learning resources.

**Understanding comes first. Confidence grows through practice.**

## 🎯 Our Learning Philosophy

DEEPEX follows a practical learning philosophy:

**Understand → Explore → Practise → Apply → Improve**

For technical learning, this means understanding a concept, exploring its syntax and examples, solving coding questions, and applying the concept.

For professional learning, it means understanding a real-life situation, learning how to respond, studying practical examples, and practising the skill.

We aim to prioritise meaningful learning over unnecessary complexity. The platform should be approachable for beginners while remaining useful as students develop their skills.

## 🗄️ Database Structure — DEEP CODE

DEEP CODE uses Firebase Firestore to store student information and programming-learning content.

The known content hierarchy is:

```text
Firestore
│
├── students
│   └── {username}
│       ├── language
│       ├── completedTopics
│       └── completedTopicsByLanguage
│
└── languages
    └── {languageId}
        └── concepts
            └── {topicId}
                └── questions
                    └── {questionId}
```

### Language-specific progress

One important improvement was separating topic completion by programming language.

The `completedTopicsByLanguage` field is designed to hold independent completion lists:

```js
{
  completedTopicsByLanguage: {
    java: [1, 2, 3],
    python: [1, 4],
    cpp: [2, 5]
  }
}
```

This prevents a topic completed in Java from automatically appearing completed in Python or C++ simply because the topic IDs match.

The older shared `completedTopics` field has been retained rather than automatically migrated or deleted.

## 🚀 Getting Started

Follow these steps to run the project locally.

### Prerequisites

Install the following before starting:

- [Node.js](https://nodejs.org/)
- npm, which is included with Node.js
- [Git](https://git-scm.com/)
- [Visual Studio Code](https://code.visualstudio.com/) or another code editor
- A Firebase project with the required Firestore configuration

### 1. Clone the repository

Replace `YOUR_GITHUB_USERNAME` and `YOUR_REPOSITORY_NAME` with your actual GitHub details.

```bash
git clone https://github.com/YOUR_GITHUB_USERNAME/YOUR_REPOSITORY_NAME.git
```

### 2. Enter the project directory

```bash
cd YOUR_REPOSITORY_NAME
```

### 3. Install dependencies

```bash
npm install
```

### 4. Configure Firebase

Ensure the Firebase configuration in the project matches your Firebase project.

Inspect `src/firebase.js` to understand how the current application initializes Firebase. If the project uses environment variables, create the required local environment file using the variable names expected by the source code.

**Important:** Do not copy real credentials into a public README or commit private configuration files. Firebase client configuration and Firebase security rules serve different purposes; configure Firestore security appropriately for the application.

### 5. Start the development server

```bash
npm run dev
```

Open the local URL printed by Vite in your browser. It is commonly `http://localhost:5173/`.

### 6. Build the production bundle

```bash
npm run build
```

### 7. Preview the production build

```bash
npm run preview
```

Use the scripts defined in `package.json` as the final authority if the commands differ in your repository.

## 🧪 Development and Testing

Development follows an iterative approach:

1. Identify a student problem or improvement opportunity.
2. Inspect the existing implementation.
3. Make a targeted change.
4. Test the functionality locally.
5. Check that existing features still work.
6. Commit and deploy when the change is ready.
7. Observe actual student usage and identify the next improvement.

For language-specific progress, useful checks include:

- Mark a topic as learned in Java.
- Verify that Java's progress updates.
- Switch to Python and C++ and verify that their progress remains independent.
- Refresh the page and verify persistence.
- Log out and log back in to verify saved data.
- Mark a topic as unread and verify that only the appropriate language changes.

Testing should cover not only whether a new feature works, but also whether it preserves existing student data and application functionality.

## 🌱 Development Roadmap

DEEPEX is being developed incrementally, with the department as the first audience.

### Phase 1 — Strengthen DEEP CODE

- Maintain the existing technical-learning experience.
- Improve usability based on actual student feedback.
- Preserve reliable language switching and independent progress tracking.
- Fix bugs and refine the user interface.

### Phase 2 — Develop DEEP SPEAK

- Finalise the five-category curriculum.
- Design a consistent bilingual lesson structure.
- Build a manageable first version of the Career Hub.
- Prioritise practical scenarios over quiz-heavy learning.
- Test lessons for clarity, usefulness, and ease of navigation.

### Phase 3 — Department MVP

- Introduce the new learning experience to approximately 150 department students.
- Observe how students use the platform.
- Collect feedback on content, language, navigation, and practice.
- Identify problems and improve the next version.

### Phase 4 — Continuous Improvement

- Refine lessons based on real usage.
- Improve accessibility and usability.
- Add content where there is a demonstrated need.
- Review data handling, reliability, and maintainability.

### Phase 5 — College-Wide Expansion

The long-term vision is to make DEEPEX useful to students across the entire college, not just Computer Science students.

This expansion will be considered after the department version has been tested and improved. Future planning may include department-specific examples, broader student access, and additional administrative capabilities.

**The immediate priority is to build something useful, validate it with real students, and expand responsibly.**

## 🔐 Privacy and Security

DEEPEX uses Firebase services to support its application functionality. Student data and learning progress should be handled responsibly.

- Do not commit passwords, private credentials, service-account keys, or secrets to the repository.
- Review Firestore security rules before production changes.
- Ensure students can access only the information and operations they are authorised to use.
- Avoid logging passwords or other sensitive information.
- Test database changes carefully and avoid destructive operations on existing student data.

The actual security posture depends on the current authentication implementation, Firebase configuration, and Firestore rules. These should be reviewed directly in the repository and Firebase Console.
